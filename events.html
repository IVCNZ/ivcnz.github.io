AIRA Conference 2026

The 5th annual Artificial Intelligence Researchers' Association Conference (AIRAC) is co-located with IVCNZ on the 23-24th of November. Make the most of your trip to Ōtepoti | Dunedin, enjoy the local hospitality over the weekend, and stay on for AIRAC after IVNCZ.
