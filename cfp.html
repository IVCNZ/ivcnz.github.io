Call for Papers

Image and Vision Computing New Zealand (IVCNZ) is New Zealand’s premier academic conference on all aspects of visual computing and related applications. Relevant topics include, but are not limited to:

  • 3D imaging
  • Artificial intelligence for computer vision
  • Astronomical imaging
  • Augmented and virtual reality
  • Biomedical imaging
  • Biologically inspired vision systems
  • Biometrics
  • Calibration techniques
  • Compressive sensing
  • Computer graphics
  • Enhancement of video and still images
  • Feature detection and extraction
  • Geometric modeling in vision and graphics
  • Hyperspectral imaging
  • Image analysis and understanding
  • Image compression and coding
  • Machine vision real-world applications
  • Medical imaging
  • Microscopy
  • Motion tracking, control, and analysis
  • Optics
  • Phase retrieval and ptychography
  • Reconstruction algorithms
  • Remote sensing
  • Rendering and scientific visualisation
  • Sonar and acoustical imaging
  • Visual pattern recognition and classification
  • X-ray imaging
