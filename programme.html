Conference Programme

IVCNZ will run from the 18th to 20th of November and will primarily be an in-person conference held in Ōtepoti | Dunedin. We are aware that for international delegates in particular the expense (both financial and environmental) of travelling may not be sustainable. The conference will be streamed online to registered attendees, and a limited number of sessions for remote presentation will be available. The programme schedule is outlined below, but is subject to change. Note that the main conference will finish around 3pm on Friday to allow for onward travel.
