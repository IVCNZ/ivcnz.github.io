Conference Programme

IVCNZ will run from the 18th to 20th of November and will primarily be an in-person conference held in Ōtepoti | Dunedin. We are aware that for international delegates in particular the expense (both financial and environmental) of travelling may not be sustainable. The conference will be streamed online to registered attendees, and a limited number of sessions for remote presentation will be available. Details of the conference programme will be announced once paper acceptance decisions have been made, but we expect to finish mid-afternoon on the Friday. This is to allow delegates who are not staying on in Ōtepoti for leisure or the AIRA conference to catch late afternoon or evening flights.
