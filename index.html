Welcome to IVCNZ 2026

Nau mai, haere mai, and welcome to the 41st International Conference on Image and Vision Computing New Zealand (IVCNZ 2026). IVCNZ covers all aspects of computer vision, image processing, computer graphics, extended reality, visualisation, and HCI applications within these fields. For more information about the topics covered by the conference, please see the Call for Papers.

IVCNZ 2026 will be held in Ōtepoti | Dunedin, New Zealand, from 18-20 November 2026. The conference will be hosted by Te Kura Rorohiko | School of Computing at Ōtākou Whakaihu Waka | The University of Otago. Dunedin is a vibrant city known for its rich history, stunning architecture, and easy access to natural wonders. For more information about the conference venue and Ōtepoti | Dunedin, see the Venue page.
